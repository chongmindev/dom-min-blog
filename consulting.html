Cafe consulting app

A free web tool that gives an independent cafe owner a one-minute diagnostic: how findable they are online, how they compare to nearby cafes, and a few concrete things to try this week.

Try the app

What it does

The owner answers a few questions (no store name, address, or phone), picks their neighborhood, and gets a report in four parts.
